A day at home

What happens by itself.

Morning

  • First step in the hallway on a weekday: SRF 3 starts quietly in bathroom and bedroom
  • From 06:45: an 8-second spoken briefing in the bathroom with weather and appointments, the train only when it is late
  • Train late by a minute or more: push "+1 Min" and the lamp blinks yellow
  • 09:00: one push with the open to-dos

Out of the house

  • Both away for 5 minutes: lights, music and TV go off
  • The dehumidifier keeps the window dry by itself
  • Dreame finished or stuck: push to both phones

Coming home

  • Dark outside: living room and hallway light up
  • Open to-dos arrive on the phone
  • Your own light scene and the wedding playlist, shuffled

Evening

  • "Filmabend": TV on, lights dim, messages wait
  • Record on: lights dim, the clock shows VINYL
  • Evening before the Karton collection, 18:30: reminder
  • Dryer done: announcement in living room and bedroom, the lamp turns white until the laundry is out
  • Good night button: everything off except the bedroom

Night

  • Hallway motion: light at 13 % for the way to the bathroom
  • The clock stays silent from 22:00 to 07:00
