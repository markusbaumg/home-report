A day at home

What happens by itself.

Morning

  • First step in the hallway on a weekday: SRF 3 starts quietly in bathroom and bedroom
  • From 06:45: an 8-second spoken briefing in the bathroom with weather and appointments, the train only when it is late
  • Train late by a minute or more: push "+1 Min · 07:18" and the lamp blinks yellow; updates stay silent
  • 09:00: one push with the open to-dos; tick them off on the dashboard

Out of the house

  • Both away for 5 minutes: lights, music and TV go off
  • The dehumidifier keeps the window dry by itself; laundry mode when clothes hang in the living room
  • Dreame finished or stuck: push to both phones

Coming home

  • Dark outside: living room and hallway light up
  • Open to-dos arrive on the phone
  • Coming home to an empty flat: your own light scene and the wedding playlist, shuffled

Evening

  • "Filmabend": TV on, lights dim, messages wait
  • Record on: lights dim, the matrix clock shows VINYL
  • Evening before the Karton collection, 18:30: reminder
  • Dryer done: announcement in living room and bedroom, the lamp turns white until the laundry is out
  • Good night button: everything off except the bedroom

Night

  • Blood sugar low: alarm on Kusi's phone; Vivi after 20 minutes below 3.3
  • Hallway motion: light at 13 % for the way to the bathroom
  • After 20 minutes below 3.3: bedroom and hallway light up dimly
  • Messages that can wait arrive at 07:00
  • The matrix clock stays silent from 22:00 to 07:00

The status lamp

The Hue Go lamp in the hallway lights up when something needs attention. The colour says what; the most important thing wins. Switch the lamp off to say "seen". In the evening it glows green as a mood light.

  • Blood sugar lowpulsesKusi's blood sugar is below 3.3.
  • Train lateblinksYour train is late and you are still at home.
  • Laundry is dryblinksThe dryer has finished and the laundry is still inside.
  • A device needs helpblinksSomething has stopped and waits for you: the dehumidifier tank is full, the vacuum is stuck or its station needs water.
  • Karton tonightPut the cardboard out, the collection is tomorrow.
  • Air the roomOpening the windows now dries the room better than the dehumidifier.
  • Plant thirstyThe soil of one of the plants is too dry.
  • Anything elseAnother open to-do, for example an empty battery.
